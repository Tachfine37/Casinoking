Navbar category links have a dropdown on hover that shows subcategories.
Structure the navbar as follows:

- Each top-level category (Casino Reviews, Slots, Poker, etc.) is hoverable
- On hover: a dark dropdown appears below with subcategory links
- Dropdown style: dark bg (#0f0f14), gold left border accent, subtle fade+slide 
  animation on appear, gold text on hover per item
- Subcategories are fetched from a /data/categories.json file for now 
  (will be replaced by Supabase categories table later)

/category/slots and /category/slots/megaways.
